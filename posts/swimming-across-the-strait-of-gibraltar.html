從歐洲「游到」非洲是什麼感覺？為什麼會有人要泳渡直布羅陀海峽？

在2023年10月15日，汶而教練僅著泳衣，無潛水衣與防寒衣，成為台灣第一位泳渡直布羅陀海峽的女生，全程6小時2分。她也是台灣使用自由式首度完成橫渡歐非的第一人！

汶而教練熱愛關於游泳、旅行與大海的一切。她藉著游泳帶大家看到不一樣的世界！懂水，你可以享受更多！本次講座機會難得，歡迎各界愛好大海的朋友報名。

What is it like to swim from Europe to Africa? Why would anyone want to swim across the Strait of Gibraltar?

On October 15th, 2023, Coach Wen became the first woman in Taiwan to swim across the Strait of Gibraltar in 6 hours and 2 minutes, wearing only a swimsuit (no wetsuit). She was also the first person in Taiwan to complete a freestyle crossing from Europe to Africa!

Coach HSU loves everything about swimming, traveling and the sea. Through swimming, she brings people to see a different world! If you know the ocean by heart, you can enjoy more! This is a great opportunity for all open water sports lovers to sign up for this unique event.

Registration: https://4sc.kktix.cc/events/coach-wen1
