Why climb stairs?

This annual event honors a Navy SEAL by climbing 1,000 steps with weight. Step-ups are tactical fitness in miniature: carry a load, move your own body, and go up.

Held in 2023 and 2024. Cancelled in 2025 because of heavy rain.
2023 recap　2024 recap

Who it's for

  • Hikers and mountaineers
  • Trail runners
  • Anyone who wants to test their fitness
  • People who must carry loads over changing terrain for long periods

What is tactical fitness?

  • Raw strength　Lift equipment or gear, or carry another person.
  • Speed endurance　Run farther and move faster under load.
  • Muscular endurance　Move up, down and through spaces with weight on your back.
  • Mobility　Move easily over uneven ground and obstacles.
  • Grip　Hold gear, climb ropes and hills, and grab things or people without tiring.
  • Tactical skills　Water survival and rescue, river crossing, meeting a boat for extraction.
  • And more　Every movement your job demands.

Reference: NSCA Tactical Strength & Conditioning

What to bring

  • Hiking boots or firm-soled shoes, with suitable socks
  • A backpack you can load with weight
  • A change of clothes. You will sweat a lot.
  • If it rains, we will announce a plan or a new venue early.

Host it somewhere else

No registration needed. Same day, same challenge, in your own city.

  1. Find a place with stairs: a park, stadium bleachers, a campus or a trailhead.
  2. Pick your level. Friendly stairs are about 20–22 cm high.
  3. Bring friends, stay within your limits, and carry water and a change of clothes.
  4. Post with #CHAD1000X to share it with everyone.
